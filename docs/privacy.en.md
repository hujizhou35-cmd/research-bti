# Privacy and data

[English](privacy.en.md) · [中文](privacy.zh-CN.md) · [日本語](privacy.ja.md)

This is a self-assessment of behavioral styles. We collect responses for statistical and research analysis of tendencies in team situations.

We collect your choices, results, question and translation versions, answer language, question and total response times, option order and positions, effort self-report, consent version and time, and a randomly generated browser identifier. We do not request names, phone numbers, government IDs or student IDs. The browser identifier helps identify repeated submissions from the same browser; it is not a unique person identifier.

Cloudflare Pages Functions receives completed responses and stores them in D1. Responses are used for aggregate statistics and research analysis; only summaries are published and responses are not sold. No automatic deletion period is currently configured.

Cloudflare hosts the site, and GeeTest provides bot verification. These services may process network requests and device information under their own privacy policies. Verification tokens are checked, but their raw contents are not stored in the response database.

Participation is voluntary. You may close the page at any time; responses are submitted only after completion. Closing the page does not delete an already submitted response. For questions or deletion requests, contact the email below. Without identifying information, we may be unable to locate a particular response.

This is a playful self-assessment, not a medical, psychological or ability assessment, or a basis for selecting people.
